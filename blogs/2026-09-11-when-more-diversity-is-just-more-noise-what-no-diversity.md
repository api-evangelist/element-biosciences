A new preprint finds that standard sequencing pipelines can mistake error for real biological diversity, and the problem gets worse the deeper you sequence. AVITI™ produced 2.5-5x fewer of these false variants than Illumina NovaSeq, suggesting accuracy, not just depth, is what determines reliable diversity estimates.
