When running libraries using Cloudbreak ™ or Cloudbreak Freestyle sequencing cartridges, typically we recommend library fragments ranging from 175 – 1000 bp. Larger library fragments (1 – 2 kb) can be sequenced on the AVITI ™ platforms but require additional considerations.
