Clinical research | Educational series Clinical research sequencing labs operate in one of the most demanding environments in genomics. These studies generate high-quality data needed to advance biological research and future clinical research applications, so accuracy and consistency are critical. The standards a lab holds their tools and partners to matters as much as the specifications on the datasheet.
